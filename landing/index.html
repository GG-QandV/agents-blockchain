🔑

Key never leaves the vault

The agent signs intents, not transactions. The wallet key stays in the vault — hardware-backed, biometric-confirmed. The agent couldn't drain even if compromised.

⚡

Zero gas fees for the owner

Payments use x402 (EIP-3009) over USDC on Base. The facilitator pays gas. The owner pays exactly the amount authorized — nothing more.

📋

Full audit trail

Every decision — allowed, denied by rule, confirmed by owner, settled, failed — is recorded in an append-only hash chain. Tamper-evident by design.
